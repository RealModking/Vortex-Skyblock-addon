# 🌌 Vortex Skyblock Addon

Welcome to **Vortex Skyblock Addon**, a feature-packed Skyblock experience for Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Build your island, expand your empire, complete challenges, and progress through custom systems designed to make Skyblock more exciting and rewarding.

## 📦 Features

### 🏝️ Skyblock Islands
- Create and manage your own island.
- Expand your island over time.
- Invite friends to join your island.
- Island permissions and management.

### 💰 Economy System
- Earn money through gameplay.
- Buy upgrades and useful items.
- Sell resources for profit.
- Balance tracking and economy integration.

### 📈 Progression
- Level up through gameplay.
- Unlock new features and rewards.
- Complete objectives to advance.

### 🎒 Backpacks
- Increase storage capacity.
- Upgrade backpack size.
- Store more resources while farming and mining.

### ⛏️ Custom Tools
- Powerful custom tools and upgrades.
- Special abilities and enhancements.
- Improve efficiency as you progress.

### 🛒 Shops
- Buy and sell items.
- Access useful resources.
- Progress through custom shop tiers.

### 🎯 Challenges
- Complete Skyblock objectives.
- Earn rewards for finishing challenges.
- Track progression through challenge systems.

### 👥 Multiplayer Support
- Play solo or with friends.
- Cooperative island gameplay.
- Shared island progression.

### ⚡ Performance Friendly
- Optimized for Realms.
- Optimized for dedicated servers.
- Designed for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

---

## 📥 Installation

1. Download the latest release.
2. Import the Behavior Pack into Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
3. Enable the addon in your world settings.
4. Make sure experimental features required by the addon are enabled.
5. Start your Skyblock adventure.
